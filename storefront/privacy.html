Privacy Policy

Information we collect

We collect account information you provide, including your name, email address, phone number, saved delivery addresses, and order information.

How we use information

We use your information to create and secure your account, process purchases, provide order updates, manage delivery, respond to enquiries, and improve the service.

Saved addresses

Saved addresses are used only to support account delivery information and order processing. You can edit or delete saved addresses from your account where account address management is available.

Data protection

We take reasonable measures to protect account and transaction information. Please keep your password private and notify us if you believe account access may be compromised.

Your choices

You may update profile details and saved addresses in your account. To ask about account data or deletion, use the contact page.
